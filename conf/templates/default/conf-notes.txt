
VisionFive 2 self-test build (oe-core + meta-riscv + meta-vf2-selftest, nodistro,
linux-mainline). Targets:

    vf2-selftest-image          minimal bootable image carrying vf2-selftest
    vf2-selftest                just the script (drop into any image)

On-board:    vf2-selftest -o /tmp/st     (KTAP on stdout; results.tsv, report.md in /tmp/st)
On hardware via oeqa (set TEST_TARGET_IP / TEST_SERVER_IP in local.conf first):
             bitbake vf2-selftest-image -c testimage
